## Summary
The adversarial wave PROVED its worth: the pen probes exposed a real spec violation — `Complete`/`Cancel` (held-family values) submitted against a consumed-escrow row were routed by the resolver to the shipped held-escrow service, whose state machine accepted a disputed consumed row (double-resolution window; a Cancel would refund an already-consumed hold's lane twice). REQ-5.2 mandates the localized `disputeResolutionMismatch` denial BEFORE any write for BOTH directions. Fix: the resolver now pre-gates the submitted outcome against the row's escrow generation (`SessionArbitrationService.assertResolutionFamilyMatchesEscrow` — probe read, disputed-row vocabulary check, localized `ValidationError(disputeResolutionMismatch)`, zero writes) before dispatching; both generation services stay byte-stable for legitimate paths, and each service still re-classifies inside its own transaction (the gate is advisory in the race sense, authoritative for the vocabulary error).
