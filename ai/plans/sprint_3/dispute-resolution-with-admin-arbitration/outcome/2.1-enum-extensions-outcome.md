## Behavior-Drift Note (carried to Task 3.1 — important)

Widening `DisputeResolution` widens what `isDisputeResolution` accepts, and the
live Pothos enum (after 3.1's regen) will offer `Refund|PartialRefund|Uphold`
on the wire. The shipped `SessionLifecycleService.resolveSessionDispute`
(`session-lifecycle.service.ts:626-646`) binary-branches `Cancel` vs
**everything-else** (`resolveDisputeCompleteOnce`), so a `Refund` member that
reaches it executes Complete semantics on held escrow. Today this is
unreachable through GraphQL (the committed SDL still parses only
`Cancel|Complete`; `isDisputeResolution`'s widened acceptance only matters for
callers that skip the boundary). **Task 3.1 must wire the resolver dispatch
(Cancel/Complete → shipped resolve; Refund/PartialRefund/Uphold →
`arbitrateDispute`) and/or add the `disputeResolutionMismatch` classification
in the same change that regenerates the schema** — do not ship the regen
without the dispatch.
