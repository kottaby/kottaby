## Adaptations Made (plan-provisional → implementation-faithful)

Per the mandate to match the REAL Drizzle `$inferSelect` shapes and the shipped
`findTransitionProbe` conventions (`backend/db/repo/classes/session.repository.ts:346`,
row type `SessionTransitionProbeRowType = Pick<SessionSelectType, …>` at
`session.types.ts:72`), the probe is a `Pick` of the select row rather than a
hand-written interface. Consequences vs the plan's provisional shape:

1. `status` is the pgEnum literal union (`"scheduled" | … | "disputed"`), NOT
   the TS `SessionStatus` enum — the schema column has no `$type<>()`
   binding (the same reason the Pothos layer maps via
   `toSessionStatus(SessionReturnType["status"])`).
2. `feeHeld` is `boolean | null` (column is nullable-with-default), not
   `boolean`.
3. `heldBalanceLane` is `HeldBalanceLane | null` via the column's
   `$type<>()` binding, not `string | null`.

`AdminDisputeCaseReturnType` keeps the plan's field names
(`session`/`report`/`homework`/`recitation`/`auditTrail`) but adopts the
layer's established read-shape convention of `readonly` members + a
`readonly` array (matching `SessionPageReturnType` and
`AdminAuditLogPageReturnType`). `ArbitrateDisputeInput` carries exactly the
plan's `arbitrateDispute(adminId, sessionId, resolution, note, partialAmount,
locale, outerTx?)` parameters minus the transaction (tx stays the trailing
service/repo parameter per conventions); `note`/`partialAmount` are explicit
`string | null` per the plan signature and the
`SessionRecitationSubmitInput.description` precedent.
