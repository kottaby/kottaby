## 1. Method (and its one limitation)

- 6 combos captured: {1440×900, 768×1024, 375×812} × {en LTR, ar RTL} → `/tmp/ui-shots/<viewport>-<locale>.png` (+ `--full` variants; 13 files incl. the F6 evidence shot).
- Locale switched through the app's real mechanism: `NEXT_LOCALE` cookie (`shared/locale/server-cookies.ts`) — same cookie the header language switcher writes.
- **Limitation recorded as a deviation:** this subagent context cannot render PNG images, so each combo was inspected through a scripted DOM/computed-style audit (grid columns, gaps, font sizes, element geometry, overflow scan, touch-target heights, `dir`/`textAlign`, computed colors) rather than by eye. The audits cover every checkpoint the task lists and are reproducible; the PNGs remain for human eyeballing. **Zero defects surfaced; zero fix iterations needed** (max budget was ~4).

- Prototype frames live in `prototype/` (desktop/tablet/ar 2560×2048 design comps, mobile 780×1854 tall scroll frame). **Pixel comparison was not possible in this context** (images not renderable by this agent — recorded as a deviation); parity was therefore assessed on the layout/flow structure the task enumerates ("translate, don't transplant"):

  - seven metric section cards on a 4→2→1 responsive grid — present and measured;
  - two 30-day trend charts side-by-side on desktop, stacked below — present (desktop grid `1fr 1fr` at lg, verified stacked at 768/375);
  - per-currency revenue table + empty-state copy, honest `—` rating placeholders — present in both locales;
  - RTL variant mirrors chrome and cards (measured alignment/geometry above) rather than merely translating text — the ar prototype's mirroring intent is honored.
- Implementation screenshots (all 6 combos) sit next to the prototype PNGs (plan dir) for the human parity eyeball; no visual defect was found that would require a fix.
