## 6. Semantic review checklist

- **Race atomicity** — Step 9a pins the deterministic loser collapse against a committed
  winner (typed `PARENT_LINK_REQUEST_ALREADY_RESOLVED` conflict, loser row swept to `expired`
  with `respondedAt` NULL, loser emits zero notifications/publishes). Step 9b runs the true
  `Promise.allSettled` two-parent confirm race with DISTINCT transports per raced call
  (loser silence proven on its own transport) and accepts both honest loser shapes (typed
  conflict or `40P01` deadlock abort via a Drizzle cause-chain walker) — both mean the loser
  committed nothing; exactly one winner link asserted. Single `withTransaction` unit per
  respond is exercised end-to-end (the DEV1-014 substrate's claim+link+sweep+emit
  atomicity is what makes 9a/9b pass).
- **Module state** — module-scope mutables are exactly the five documented suite-scoped
  registries (`state`, `createdRequestIds`, `raceUserIds`, `transportSpy`, `tracked`);
  every cross-step row reference goes through `requireRequestId`/`requireState` keyed
  handles (select BY ID, never by list position); no `beforeAll`-independent mutable logic.
- **Env-config** — no hardcoded URLs/secrets; TTL/window math comes from
  `@/shared/constants/parent-link-request.constants`; no clock/currency/endpoint literals.
- **Dead code** — none (oxlint + type-aware clean; every helper/oracle declared is used).
- **Cross-layer** — zero `@/frontend` / `@/app` imports (rg verified); imports are
  bun:test/drizzle + `@/backend/**` services-repos-enums-errors-types + `@/shared/**` +
  `@/test/**` helpers only.

  notification-type comparison constant is typed as the DB column's primitive union (no
  unsafe enum comparison); zero raw status string literals; `PARENT_LINK_RELATED_ENTITY_TYPE`
  is the sanctioned free-varchar drawer key (no enum exists — per 0.2 §1).
- **Scope boundary** — tests-only delta; services/repos/resolvers consumed verbatim; no
  `runInRollback` (services spawn their own top-level transactions), no `mock.module`, no
  `console.*`, no `any` casts, no `expect(...).rejects.toThrow()` (try/catch via
  `catchJourneyError`).

  `provision*Actor` factories; G is genuinely suspended (`setGovernanceFixture`), L is
