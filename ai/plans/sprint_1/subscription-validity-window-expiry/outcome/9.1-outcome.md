## Recurring patterns & gotchas (extracted from outcome/ 0.1→8.1 + reviews + research)

1. **The guarded single-statement transition is the repo's universal concurrency arbiter** — `activatePendingOnce`, `expireDueActive`, `zeroLaneIfNoCoveringSubscription`, `decrementLaneIfAvailable` all fuse predicate + mutation in one statement under the row lock (TOCTOU = 0); zero rows = replay/no-op, never an error. New state-machine writers must follow this doctrine or add a DB guard backstop (INV-PAY2 precedent).
2. **One clock reading per aggregate, one transaction per aggregate** — `now` is captured exactly once inside the sweep/activation transaction; the whole cohort commits or rolls back together (failure domain = the transaction, not the row).
3. **Cron surfaces are fail-closed and existence-oracle-free** — env gates FIRST, bare 404 (no envelope, no content-type) when disabled, timing-safe SHA-256 digest bearer, masked envelopes otherwise; the bare 404 is the single sanctioned taxonomy-exempt status literal, documented on the route itself.
4. **Closed vocabularies over defensive checks** — frozen enum-keyed column maps (`ZERO_LANE_BALANCE_COLUMNS`, `HELD_LANE_TO_CREDIT_LANE`, `CREDIT_LANE_BALANCE_COLUMNS`) make wrong-lane writes compile-time impossible and give structural exemptions for free (trial has no member); unreachable states abort LOUD (`logger.error` + cohort rollback) instead of silently doing nothing.
5. **Honest counts-only contracts cross the wire** — `{ expired, lanesZeroed }` from guarded statements; replays report honest zeroes; no row ids, no PII in logs (ids/counts only).
6. **Where exactness is unimplementable, choose the conservative direction and record the residual** — O1 conditional lane zeroing under-revokes (Revoke-Never-Wrongly) on shared-lane co-subscriptions; the attribution ledger is the recorded future refinement, not a silently accepted gap.
7. **Failure-branch gating = zero hot-path cost** — the booking expiry gate lives strictly in the double-debit-miss branch; the successful path gains zero reads; structural exemptions (trial-first ladder) need no extra checks.
8. **Test-first journeys caught the real integration gaps** — the Phase-3 RED journey drove 5.1/5.2; the 4-tier suites + route/env-gymnastics + locale parity auto-coverage formed the verification spine (all green at 8.1's gate).
