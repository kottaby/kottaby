### SEC — Security & Tenancy Audit (explicit verdicts)

- **`$all` conjunction is load-bearing (verified at the plugin source):** `builder.ts` does NOT set `scopeAuthOptions.defaultStrategy` (grep zero hits), and `@pothos/plugin-scope-auth` defaults to ANY semantics — `node_modules/@pothos/plugin-scope-auth/esm/request-cache.js:324`: `this.defaultStrategy = builder.options.scopeAuth?.defaultStrategy ?? 'any'`. A plain `{ authenticated: true, role: [UserRole.Teacher] }` map would therefore grant ANY authenticated caller (first satisfied leg wins) — the mutation's `$all` wrapper forces the ALL conjunction (`request-cache.js:254`: `evaluateScopeMap(map, info, forAll = this.defaultStrategy === 'all')`). Net: anonymous → `UNAUTHORIZED` (401) pre-resolver; authenticated non-teacher → `FORBIDDEN` (403) pre-resolver (builder.ts `unauthorizedError` mapping keeps DomainError codes verbatim).
- **Query scope authenticated-only, tenancy service-owned:** `{ authenticated: true }` walls off anonymous callers only; every role that passes (student/parent/admin/teacher) reaches the resolver and the service's DB-row participant predicate (`teacherId === caller || studentId === caller`) owns the entire decision, collapsing foreign ≡ nonexistent ≡ malformed ≡ not-yet-recorded into the identical `null` (no existence oracle).
- **Closed input whitelist (BOPLA):** `SessionRecitationInput` carries exactly `name` + `description`; the resolver maps field-by-field with `?? null` normalization — no spread of `args.input` (grep: the only `{ ...args.input }` occurrence is the prohibition comment), no identity passthrough, no server-controlled field (row id, owning session, timestamps) is input-bound. Smuggled fields die as `GRAPHQL_VALIDATION_FAILED` pre-resolver.
- **`ctx.user.id` is the sole identity source:** neither op accepts any identity argument; `sessionId` is the only client-supplied coordinate and is re-validated by the service (positive-safe pre-DB; ownership/participation from DB rows only).
