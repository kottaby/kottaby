## Cross-File Dependencies (for 6.1 / 9.3)

1. **6.1 purchase flow order (all ONE tx):** `insertClaim({ idempotencyKey, userId })` → subscription/payment/junction inserts → `updateClaimSubscriptionId(claim.id, subscription.id)`. The 23505 from a racing duplicate joins the caller's own error translation: same-caller ⇒ `ConflictError("DUPLICATE_REQUEST")`, foreign caller ⇒ oracle-safe `NotFoundError("PAYMENT", …)` (session-lifecycle precedent) — the repo intentionally stays translation-free.
2. **Replay branch:** committed-claim lookup is `findByKey(key)`; ownership comparison (claim.userId === caller) is the service's decision.
3. The claim outlives its subscription (`subscription_id` is set-null on delete) so replays after deletion still surface duplicate semantics — do not "clean up" claims when subscriptions are removed.
4. GraphQL replay tests (9.3) assert 409 `DUPLICATE_REQUEST`; they can rely on the raw 23505 being the ONLY failure mode of a duplicate `insertClaim` inside the purchase tx (constraint name pinned in this suite).
