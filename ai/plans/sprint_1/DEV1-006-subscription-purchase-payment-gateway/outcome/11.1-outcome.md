## Notification interception seam (documented)

The activation service publishes strictly post-commit through
`NotificationEngine.publishReceipts` and exposes **no injected-transport parameter** —
so the suite spies that exact namespace-bound publish boundary (`spyOn` from `bun:test`,
module-scope install, restored in `afterAll`), captures the receipts, and asserts BOTH
that a publish happened and exactly which userIds it targeted (`[Student A]` only, once,
never for replays/failed legs). The notification ROW itself persists for real and is
tracked for teardown. This is the layer's sanctioned interception rule applied where the
service's seam actually is.

## Residue policy (immutable ledger)

- `student_payments` rows are append-only (BEFORE DELETE trigger) — but deletable under
  the sanctioned `withImmutabilityTriggersSuspended(["student_payments"])` teardown
  suspension. `afterAll` runs that leg FIRST (payments by explicit tracked id list),
  then sweeps the composite-PK junction rows by subscription id (no registry entry —
  re-probed inline for zero residue), then `TrackedFixtures.cleanup()` hard-deletes
  everything else in reverse registration order (claims → subscriptions → plan →
  notifications → role-children → users) and re-probes EVERY registered row.
- Teardown leaves ZERO residue (proven by the three consecutive green runs — a second
  green run is impossible with any leak). Unique per-run prefixes make any mid-crash
  residue greppable and collision-free.
