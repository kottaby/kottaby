## 6. Carry-forward knowledge for 3.x / 4.x / 5.x

- **The census is the journey's action list.** Task 5.1 executes every `wired` row through the real service path; do not hand-maintain a parallel step list.
- `setPlanActiveStatus` → `Suspend` (deactivate) / `Reactivate` (activate) on entityType `plan`; there is no dedicated plan-status verb.
- `resolveSessionDispute` → `Override` on entityType `session` (one row per arbitration, regardless of resolution direction).
- `adminSetUserDeleted`, `adminSetUserSuspended`, `adminSetUserBlocked` are direction-paired: expect exactly one row per call (Delete **or** Reactivate; Suspend **or** Reactivate), chosen by the boolean arg.
- `Adjust` is fixture-only: no shipped producer. The journey mints it through the System fixture lane (`db.insert(auditLogs)` with the `rawActionType` helper under `withAuditDeleteTriggersSuspended`), which is why `ACTION_TYPE_COVERAGE[AuditActionType.Adjust] === "fixture"`.
- Observer reads go through `AuditTrailService.listAuditTrail` with its filter axes (actor/actionType/entityType/entityId/time window); canonical ordering is `createdAt DESC, id DESC` for same-timestamp tiebreaks.
- Wiring phases 3.x/4.x must keep emissions exactly on the shapes pinned here (Create/plan, Update/plan, Suspend|Reactivate/plan, Override/session) or the bijection/coverage assertions fail.
