### BS-D1 defect (found → fixed → re-verified)

- **Defect:** MUI v9's `Badge` anchor is PHYSICAL (`inset` + translate CSS vars set inline on the badge slot; verified against `node_modules/@mui/material/Badge/Badge.js` — no `direction` handling). Live probe under `dir=rtl` (1440×900, badge "99+"): badgeCenterX 268.5 == icon **RIGHT** edge — identical to LTR, i.e. anchored at the bell's top-START under RTL, NOT the runbook's "top-end, RTL auto-mirrored" contract (evidence: `defect-rtl-badge-anchor-desktop-ar.png`).
- **Fix (sx-only):** `[dir=rtl] & .MuiBadge-badge` override in `NotificationUnreadBadge.tsx` flipping `--Badge-inset: 0 auto 0 0`, `--Badge-origin: 0% 0%`, `--Badge-translate: -50%, -50%` (`!important` required to beat the inline slot style; the app never sets `theme.direction`, so `[dir=rtl]` on `<html>` is the seam).
- **Re-verified:** post-fix reload → RTL badgeCenterX 244 == icon LEFT edge at 1440×900 AND 124 == iconLeft at 768×1024; LTR unchanged (1196 == iconRight); full matrix re-captured on the fixed anchor (`overflow-desktop-ar.png`, `appbar-badge-*-ar.png`).
- Note: the pre-fix `overflow-desktop-ar.png` was preserved as `defect-rtl-badge-anchor-desktop-ar.png` (defect → fix story in one evidence set).
